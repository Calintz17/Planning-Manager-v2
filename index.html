How it works

From your rules to a planning in three steps

1

Set your rules

Opening hours, shifts, breaks, corners, contracts and minimum staffing. Saved automatically.

2

Add your team and traffic

Advisors, leaves and days off, then traffic or workload by month, day and hour.

3

Review and share

CAST builds the month and each day. Change any cell, then download in Excel or PDF, or send it.

Principles

A tool that helps, never one that blocks

You have the last word

Manual changes are always kept, even when the planning is recalculated.

Rules come first

Working days, days in a row, weekly hours and rest between shifts, as set for your store.

Best with who is there

Short on people? CAST plans the best possible day and leaves the final call to the manager.

Desktop and phone

Read and edit the planning anywhere. Tap menus on mobile, drag controls on desktop.
